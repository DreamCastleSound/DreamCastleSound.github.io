# What's a Dream Castle?
... A Dream Castle is... A combination of various ideas. A synthesis of internal exploration  through creative works, the concept of memory palaces to represent ideas, and the Jungian pop psychology idea that one can be represented in a dream as an ever expanding "house" of sorts. In some mystical practices, as well as contemporary self help, one might design a "memory palace" when learning new things, in order to help organize their thoughts as well as to recall things they'd like to remember across disparate subjects. It's an imaginative mental mnemonic device, leveraging your visual imagination and proprioception to create recall triggers for individual facts and details by categories of your choosing. 

Often, one builds this fantasy palace with different rooms, or even whole wings and courtyards, for different concepts and ideas. An example might be a vast ornate observatory for astronomy; wherein the designer imagines and plans a detailed description of a vast ornate room with individual work and research stations, telescopes, globes, star charts, and other visually distinct and easy to imagine details which all represent different topics under the subheading/room "astronomy." Or a gorgeous sprawling library with different distinct sections and reading nooks for individual stories. A concert hall. A music room. A well stocked pantry for grandma's old recipes. These rooms may start out rather bare at first -- it wouldn't do one any good to stuff their head full of useless trinkets without an idea bonded to them. But over time, as one studies a concept and creates associative images alongside it -- a new workstation or a new desk object for each important detail -- the memory palace grows and becomes more populated. A psychic imprint which can be explored imaginatively. If one possesses sufficient powers of imagination and command over their night time dreams (I envy you~ ) then this palace may even be explored as a physical place in a lucid dream! 

This practice goes hand in hand with a Jungian concept; the idea that dreams about certain locations can represent your sense of self. Usually this would be one's home, imagined, remembered, or felt intuitively in a way only dream-logic can make any sense out of. It is a common experience among artists and folks coming of age, to imagine or have dreamt of a house representing their own home -- perhaps newly renovated, freshly revisited after a long journey, or newly theirs by inheritance or purchase -- to find their familiar abode changed. Different. A new upstairs vast as a shopping mall. An elevator in a suburban residential home -- who knows where it leads. A dusty, long abandoned backrooms liminal space of half constructed crawlspaces, basements, unpainted rooms, or any other manner of curious nooks, crannies, or total void spaces. The dreamer may have a sense of dread at this new revelation. Or awe and wonder. Or, most common in my personal experience, intrigue and curiosity. A sense of exploration. Jungian pop psychology says the home or dreamspace in these instances represents the artist's (or expanding mind's) own self; consistent, but somehow always changing. Each new corridor a representation of growing understanding; new adventures in life. Or integrating previous experiences with current imaginings of their work or personality. It's a fascinatingly subjective paracosm to explore. To conceive of oneself as being both a pinpoint human observer, and the world around themselves being observed... 

# So, about that blog title then... 
I've long had the idea brewing, that artistic endeavors lend themselves to self discovery and introspection, even if one's creative outlet is less commonly recognized as art. It could be mathematics, chemistry, medicine, creative writing, sports, self care... Or signals and system's theory paired with musical intention. These personal hobbies, these expressions of one's personally curated understanding of a topic, all contribute to one's larger life experience, and their understanding of themselves and the world in and around them. This is especially true, I think, of creative writing, music, poetry, and theater. (But doubtless, I am leaving out many avenues for this kind of introspection!) With each new creative work, each stroke of a pen, one lays the bricks for their own inner mansion. Their memory palace. 

To dream and to create, and to imagine and to learn. The foundations of building one's next jumping off point. The next project. The next collaboration. The next chapter of one's own existence, and indeed, the existence of every mind their work touches. This, is the making of one's Dream Castle. The internal result of their created work, and the enhanced ability to imagine and dream up newer, more fantastic landscapes of sound, numbers, logic, colorful contrast, drama. That's what this little corner of the internet and its pretentious sounding author aims to be. The foundations and documenting of building my own Dream Castle, as it relates to sound, synthesis, signals and systems. 

I don't know when it will grow beyond this rambling readme. But hopefully this represents the seed of a new wonderland full of rich sonic possibilities, and sonic geekery. Dream along with me. The next creative thing done is another brick laid. It's good work to be doing.
